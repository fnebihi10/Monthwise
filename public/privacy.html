Privacy & data

Updated October 28, 2026. Monthwise is operated by Fatlind Nebihi. Contact: nebigifatlind@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Monthwise uses your Google account identifier and verified email for sign-in. It stores the accounts, balances, transactions, transfers, notes, budgets, planned bills, savings goals and allocations you enter. It does not connect to your bank or request access to Google Drive, Gmail or your contacts.

Where records go

Google handles sign-in. Cloudflare hosts the app and its database. Requests necessarily include network information such as your IP address; hashed IP-based counters help limit sign-in abuse. Google Fonts supplies the interface fonts. Monthwise does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Financial records are scoped to your signed-in account. The service operator and hosting provider can administer the database; the ledger is not end-to-end encrypted.

Cookies and offline copies

Protected session cookies keep you signed in for up to seven days. A short-lived cookie protects the Google sign-in process. Browser storage keeps an offline ledger copy and pending entries on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser may access local data. Sign out on shared devices and clear site data when handing a device to someone else. Browser storage is not a backup.

Export and deletion

Download your data from Backup & app. You can delete your cloud account and ledger there after a fresh Google sign-in. This revokes all sessions and clears the current browser's private cache. Clear site data on other devices to remove their offline copies. Cloudflare recovery backups may retain previous database versions until their retention period expires. Downloaded files stay wherever you saved them.

Availability and limits

The public version is an initial release. Each ledger supports 25,000 total records and JSON backups up to 8 MB. Requests are rate limited to protect the service. Keep independent backups. Monthwise tracks the information you enter; it does not provide banking or investment services.

Contact the operator for privacy questions or problems accessing export or deletion.
